While your lights were in storage, Remote Falcon got a lot better. Highlights
from this year:

* A public show map — visitors can find shows near them at
https://remotefalcon.com/map. It's opt-in, with separate switches for other
Remote Falcon operators and for the public.
* A rebuilt dashboard — live show health, plugin heartbeat history, and quick
actions in one place.
* Audience analytics (beta) — concurrent viewers, dwell time, new vs returning,
and your season regulars, now with custom date ranges and CSV export.
* A QR code generator — yard-sign-ready codes that put your viewer page one
scan away.
* Smarter PSAs — more control over announcements between songs, including
leader sequences and on-demand overrides.
* Sequence groups & categories — organize a big song library the way your show
actually runs.
* Fairness controls — vote caps per viewer, per-night play limits, no
back-to-back repeats within a category, and already-played songs grayed out
instead of quietly rejected.
* A second chance at the location prompt — on shows that require viewers to be
nearby, anyone who dismissed the browser's location prompt was stuck. They now
get a way to turn it back on and request a song.
* A faster viewer page — artwork and fonts no longer hold up the page, so it
comes up quicker on a phone at the curb.
* Artist and album art, filled in for you — one click looks up a sequence (or
your whole library at once) and fills in the artist and cover art.
* A documented public API — the public API now ships a full OpenAPI spec, and
show details include each sequence's artwork.
* Two-factor authentication — optional, and worth turning on.

Your five-minute preseason checklist
Now that you've heard about all the cool new stuff, we'd like to make sure
you're ready to go for the season. So here's a quick checklist to get started.

1. Sign in and look around — the dashboard will show you anything that needs
attention.
2. Update the FPP plugin on your controller and confirm the dashboard shows
Connected.
3. Sync your sequences so this year's playlist is in and last year's leftovers
are out.
4. Refresh your viewer page — new season, new look (templates make it quick).
5. Put your show on the map — two switches on the Shows Map page decide who
sees you: other Remote Falcon operators, everyone, or neither.
6. Turn on two-factor auth in Account Settings. Two minutes, once.
